# UX SPECIFICATION

## Project: Janhvi × Krish Interactive Save-the-Date

### UX Specification v1.0

---

# 1. PRODUCT PURPOSE

This is an interactive digital Save-the-Date.

It is NOT a conventional wedding invitation.

The experience should initially feel like an unfamiliar digital system rather than a wedding announcement.

The guest gradually discovers:

**19–20 November 2026 · Jaipur**

and eventually understands that the system is announcing:

**Janhvi × Krish**

The experience ends by asking the guest to respond:

- YES
- MAYBE
- NO

The Save-the-Date does not provide detailed wedding information.

---

# 2. INFORMATION THAT MAY BE SHOWN

The guest-facing experience may reveal only:

- Janhvi
- Krish
- 19 November 2026
- 20 November 2026
- November 2026
- Jaipur

No other wedding logistics should be exposed.

---

# 3. INFORMATION THAT MUST NOT BE SHOWN

Do not show:

- venue
- venue address
- wedding functions
- Mehendi
- Haldi
- Sangeet
- ceremony timings
- itinerary
- accommodation
- travel information
- dress code
- meal information
- event-specific RSVP
- guest list
- household information
- other invited guests
- plus-one entitlement
- additional guest management

Those details will be communicated later through the actual wedding invitation.

---
